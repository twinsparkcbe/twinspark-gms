1. Introduction
Twinspark Tyres And Bike Garage is a premium motorcycle tyre retailer and professional service centre located in
Coimbatore, serving performance and sports motorcycles such as Yamaha, KTM, BMW and Kawasaki. Alongside track
tyres and brand-new tyres, the business sells engine oil, chains, sprocket kits, brake parts, lubricants, accessories and
other spare parts, and runs a full-service motorcycle garage.
This proposal outlines a web-based Garage Management System designed to digitize and unify Twinspark's inventory,
purchase, sales, service and billing operations, replacing the current manual record-keeping with a single, centralized
platform.


2. Business Problem
Inventory, sales and service records are currently maintained manually, making it difficult to track stock accuracy,
monitor purchase and sales performance, and get a real-time view of business health. Twinspark needs a centralized
system to manage inventory, purchases, sales, garage services, invoicing, profit tracking, online tyre orders and
business reporting.


3. Project Objective
The objective is to develop a single web application that helps Twinspark manage Inventory, Purchases, Sales, Service
Jobs, Billing, Customers, Online Orders and Reports — reducing manual work while improving inventory accuracy and
overall business visibility.

5. Key Functional Highlights
Inventory & Purchases
 • Track Tyres are managed without category or brand; Brand New Tyres require both.
 • Purchase price and selling price are always stored separately.
 • Stock increases automatically on purchase entry and decreases on sales or service usage.

Sales & Fitting
 • A single Sales module handles track tyres, brand-new tyres and all other inventory items.
 • Optional tyre fitting at ₹300 per wheel, itemized separately from product cost.

Service & Job Cards
 • Every service generates a digital Job Card capturing customer, vehicle and odometer details.
 • Supports predefined General Service packages as well as Specific (individual) services.
 • Inventory consumed during service (oil, brake pads, chains, etc.) is automatically deducted and itemized on the
   invoice.

Billing & Invoicing
 • Sales and Service share a common billing engine with optional GST and discount.
 • Labour charges appear only on Service invoices; fitting charges appear only on Sales invoices.

Online Track Tyre Orders
 • Customers order via a public link, submitting contact details, address, PIN code, quantity and a payment
   screenshot.
 • Admin verifies payment, approves and dispatches orders; stock reduces only after dispatch.
 • Admin can export a single printable PDF of courier labels for multiple selected orders.


6. User Roles & Access
 Role                       Access

 Administrator              Full system access — Dashboard, Inventory, Purchases, Sales, Service, Reports,
                            Online Orders, Settings and User Management.

 Sales Person               Restricted access — can create sales invoices, verify and dispatch online orders, and
                            generate courier labels. No access to Inventory, Purchases, Reports, Dashboard
                            analytics or Settings.

8. Key Business Rules
 •   Purchase Price and Selling Price are always stored separately.
 •   Track Tyres do not require Category or Brand; Brand New Tyres require both.
 •   Stock automatically increases after Purchase and decreases after Sales or Service usage.
 •   Every Sale and every Service generates an invoice; every Service also generates a Job Card.
 •   GST and Discount are optional on all invoices.
 •   Fitting Charges apply only to Sales invoices; Labour Charges apply only to Service invoices.
 •   Online Orders reduce inventory only after the order is dispatched.


9. Deliverables
 •   A fully functional, responsive web application covering all modules listed in Section 4.
 •   Role-based login for Administrator and Sales Person users.
 •   Automated invoice and job-card generation (print/PDF ready).
 •   Public online order form with admin approval and dispatch workflow.
 •   Courier label PDF export for online orders.
 •   Dashboard and reporting suite as described in Sections 5 and 7.
 •   Basic user documentation for Admin and Sales Person roles.


10. Project Scope Summary
In Scope: Dashboard, Inventory Management, Purchase Management, Sales Management, Service Management,
Customer & Vehicle Management, Billing & Invoice Generation, Job Card Management, Online Track Tyre Orders,
Courier Label PDF Export, Reports, and User Role Management.
Any requirements outside the modules and rules described in this document — for example third-party accounting
integration, SMS/WhatsApp notifications, or multi-branch support — are considered out of scope for this phase and
can be discussed as future enhancements.


11. Conclusion
This Garage Management System will give Twinspark Tyres And Bike Garage a single, centralized platform to run day-
to-day operations — from inventory and purchases to sales, service and online tyre orders — while giving
management clear, real-time visibility into revenue and profit. We look forward to partnering with Twinspark to
deliver this solution.
