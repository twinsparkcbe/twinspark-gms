Twinspark Tyres And Bike Garage — Garage Management
System
Full Functional & Data Specification (Single Source of Truth)
      Purpose of this document: This is the complete, self-contained specification for the
      Twinspark Garage Management System. It is meant to be used as the single reference
      context when generating code (e.g. in Claude Code) — every module, field, business
      rule, role permission, invoice format, and report is documented here so no additional
      context should be required.



1. Project Overview
Client: Twinspark Tyres And Bike Garage Location: Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu Business
Type: Premium motorcycle tyre retailer + professional motorcycle service center, serving
performance/sports bikes (Yamaha, KTM, BMW, Kawasaki, and other premium brands).
Core Business Lines:
     1. Selling Track Tyres
     2. Selling Brand New Tyres and other motorcycle products
     3. Motorcycle Service & Garage Operations

Other Inventory Sold: Engine Oil, Chains, Chain Sprocket Kits, Brake Parts, Lubricants,
Accessories, Other Motorcycle Spare Parts.
Goal: Replace manual inventory/sales/service record-keeping with a single centralized web
application covering Inventory, Purchases, Sales, Service Jobs, Billing, Customers, Online
Orders, and Reports — improving inventory accuracy and business visibility while
reducing manual work.

4.1 Authentication & User Roles
     Two roles only: Administrator and Sales Person.
     Login via mobile number/username + password (define exact mechanism during
     build).
     Role determines visible modules and available actions (see permission matrix in
     Section 6).

4.2 Dashboard
Metrics displayed:
     Current Track Tyre Stock (quantity)
     Total Sales (count and/or amount — clarify with client which is meant)
     Purchase Amount (aggregate, likely current month/period)
     Sales Amount (aggregate)
     Profit → Profit = Sales Amount − Purchase Amount
     Current Month Revenue
     Low Stock Alerts — auto-populated list of Inventory Items where
     available_quantity ≤ low_stock_threshold


Access: Administrator only (Sales Person has no Dashboard analytics access per role
rules).

4.3 Inventory Management
     Lists all inventory items across all types (Track Tyres, Brand New Tyres, Engine Oil,
     Chains, Sprocket Kits, Brake Parts, Lubricants, Accessories, Other Spare Parts).
     Add/Edit inventory item form:
           If item_type = TRACK_TYRE → hide Category and Brand fields.
           If item_type = BRAND_NEW_TYRE → require Category and Brand selection.
          Always capture Purchase Price and Selling Price as two separate fields.
          Available Quantity is read-only on this screen — it only changes via Purchase
          Entries, Sales, or Service usage, never edited directly (except perhaps an Admin-
          only stock correction/audit feature, if desired — flag as an open question).
     Access: Administrator only.

4.4 Purchase Management
     "Add Purchase Entry" form: select Inventory Item → enter Purchase Quantity → enter
     Purchase Price → Save.
    On save: available_quantity increases automatically; a Purchase history record is
    stored (never deleted/overwritten — full audit trail).
    Purchase History view: filterable list of all past purchase entries (by item, date range,
    etc.).
    Access: Administrator only.

4.5 Sales Management
    Single unified Sales screen for all inventory types (Track Tyres, Brand New Tyres,
    Engine Oil, Chains, Sprockets, Accessories, etc.).
    Flow:
      1. Enter/select Customer (Name, Mobile Number, Address) — auto-suggest
         existing customers by mobile number.
      2. Add one or more Sale Items:
               Track Tyre: select item, enter quantity only (no category/brand prompts).
               Brand New Tyre: select Category → select Brand → select specific item →
               enter quantity. Selling Price auto-pulled from Inventory.
               Other items (oil, chains, sprockets, accessories, etc.): select item, enter
               quantity, selling price auto-pulled from Inventory.
      3. Optionally mark Fitting Required and specify number of wheels → system
         computes Fitting Charge at ₹300/wheel, shown as a distinct line, never merged
         into product subtotal.
      4. Optionally apply GST.
      5. Optionally apply Discount.
      6. System computes Subtotal → + Fitting Charges → + GST − Discount → Grand
         Total.
      7. On completing the sale: inventory auto-deducts for every sold item; a Sales
         Invoice is generated.
    Access: Administrator and Sales Person (both can create sales).

4.6 Service Management
    Flow:
      1. Capture/select Customer (Name, Mobile Number, Address).
      2. Capture/select Vehicle (Vehicle Number, Vehicle Model) and current Odometer
         Reading.
      3. Choose Service Type:
             General Service: select a predefined package (e.g. contains Oil Change,
             Water Wash, Standard Inspection, etc.); package includes a Service Charge;
             Labour Charge entered separately.
             Specific Service: select one or more individual services (Oil Change, Brake
             Service, Water Wash, Chain Cleaning, etc.); Labour Charge entered
                separately per job.
       4. Record Service Inventory Usage: staff select any inventory items consumed
          (Engine Oil, Chains, Brake Pads, Tyres, Accessories) with quantities — auto-
          deducted from stock and itemized on the invoice separately from service/labour
          charges.
       5. Job Card is generated automatically (see 4.7) capturing all of the above plus a
          free-form line-item table.
       6. Optionally apply GST and/or Discount.
       7. Generate Service Invoice.
     Access: Administrator (full); Sales Person has no access to Service Management per
     role rules (not listed among Sales Person permissions).

4.7 Job Card
     Every Service entry automatically produces a Digital Job Card containing:
          Customer Details
          Vehicle Details
          A line-item table: No | Description | Amount — staff can add multiple rows
          freely.
          Auto-calculated totals.
     Printable/exportable, tied 1:1 with its Service Job and its resulting Invoice.

4.8 Customer Management
     Central customer directory: Name, Mobile Number, Address.
     Auto-created/reused whenever a Sale or Service references a mobile number.
     Should support search/filter by name or mobile number, and show a customer's full
     sales + service history.

4.9 Vehicle Management
     Tracks vehicles per customer: Vehicle Number, Vehicle Model, Odometer Reading
     history.
     Supports one customer having multiple vehicles.
     Useful for service history lookups (e.g. "show all past services for KA-01-XXXX").

4.10 Billing & Invoice Management
Both Sales and Service share one billing engine, but render two distinct invoice layouts:
Sales Invoice contains:
     Products (line items with quantity, unit price, line total)
     Tyre Fitting Charges (separate line, only if applicable)
     GST (optional)
     Discount (optional)
     Grand Total

Service Invoice contains:
     Service Charges (from General Service package, if applicable)
     Labour Charges (always separate line)
     Inventory Used (separate itemized section — parts/consumables consumed during
     service)
     GST (optional)
     Discount (optional)
     Grand Total

Strict separation rules:
     Fitting Charges appear only on Sales Invoices, never Service.
     Labour Charges appear only on Service Invoices, never Sales.
     GST and Discount are optional toggles on every invoice type.

4.11 Online Track Tyre Orders
     Customer-facing (public link, no login required):
         Submit: Name, Mobile Number, Address, PIN Code, Quantity of Track Tyres,
         Payment Screenshot upload.
         Order enters system with status SUBMITTED .
     Admin/Sales Person workflow:
      1. View incoming orders (list/queue).
      2. Verify Payment Screenshot → mark PAYMENT_VERIFIED .
      3. Approve Order → APPROVED .
       4. Dispatch Order → DISPATCHED — this is the only point at which Track Tyre
          stock is decremented.
     Courier Label Export: select one or more orders (typically approved/dispatched) →
     export a single printable PDF containing one label per order (Customer Name, Mobile
     Number, Address, PIN Code, Quantity), ready to attach to courier packages.
     Access: Administrator (full workflow) and Sales Person (verify, approve/dispatch,
     generate labels — per role rules, Sales Person "Can: Verify Online Orders, Dispatch
     Orders, Generate Courier Labels").

5. Consolidated Business Rules (Checklist)
 1. Purchase Price and Selling Price are always stored as two separate fields — never one
    derived from the other.
 2. Track Tyres do not require Category or Brand.
 3. Brand New Tyres require both Category and Brand.
 4. Stock automatically increases after a Purchase Entry.
 5. Stock automatically decreases after a completed Sale.
 6. Stock automatically decreases when inventory is consumed during a Service.
 7. Every Service generates a Job Card.
 8. Every Sale generates an Invoice.
 9. Every Service generates an Invoice.
10. GST is optional on every invoice.
11. Discount is optional on every invoice.
12. Tyre Fitting Charges appear only on Sales invoices (never Service).
13. Labour Charges appear only on Service invoices (never Sales).
14. Fitting Charge rate: ₹300 per wheel, always itemized separately from product cost.
15. Online Orders reduce inventory only after Dispatch — not at submission, payment
    verification, or approval.
16. Profit is calculated as: Profit = Sales Amount − Purchase Amount.
17. Low-stock items automatically surface on the Dashboard (threshold-based).

7. Invoice Numbering & Format Notes
     Recommend a unique, sequential invoice number scheme, e.g. TW-S-000123 (Sales)
     and TW-J-000123 (Service/Job) to distinguish invoice types at a glance — confirm
     preferred prefix with client.
     Invoices should be printable and exportable as PDF.
     Every invoice must clearly separate: Subtotal → Additional Charges (Fitting or
     Labour, as applicable) → GST (if applicable) → Discount (if applicable) → Grand Total.
